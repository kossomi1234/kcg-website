About KCG

Kossomi CyberGuard is a Chadian cybersecurity organization dedicated to making the digital world safer for everyone. We combine education, hands-on training and practical security services to help people and organizations understand threats, defend against them and stay secure.

LearnCybersecurity knowledge made clear and practical.
ProtectDefensive skills and services to safeguard what matters.
Stay SecureLasting habits and support for a safer digital life.

Our Courses & Services

Six ways KCG helps you learn, protect and stay secure.

1

Cybersecurity Education

Structured courses that take learners from the fundamentals to real-world security skills.

2

Digital Safety Awareness

Practical sessions on phishing, passwords, scams and safe online habits for everyday users.

3

Ethical Hacking Training

Learn to think like an attacker, legally and responsibly, in order to defend better.

4

Network Security

Securing networks and systems so organizations can reduce risk and stay resilient.

5

Research & Innovation

Local research and new ideas that address Chad’s own cybersecurity challenges.

6

Consulting Services

Expert guidance to assess your security, plan improvements and build a safer organization.
